Generative AI for Next-Generation Business Analytics

Author: Pushkar Gupta

Journal: Hampton Global Business Review (2025/10/21)

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24487633

Abstract

Generative artificial intelligence (AI) is redefining the landscape of business analytics by enabling machines to produce novel content—ranging from text and images to synthetic data—that enhances decision-making, personalization, and operational efficiency. This article explores the transformative potential of generative AI technologies, particularly large language models (LLMs), generative adversarial networks (GANs), and diffusion models, in driving the next wave of innovation in analytics. Through detailed examination of applications across key sectors—including finance, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, and media—the study illustrates how generative AI augments traditional analytics by synthesizing insights, simulating scenarios, and automating complex workflows. The article also discusses the strategic, organizational, and ethical implications of adopting generative AI in business contexts, highlighting challenges related to accuracy, bias, data governance, and compliance. With a forward-looking perspective, it argues that integrating generative AI into business analytics will not only boost productivity and innovation but also require organizations to rethink skill development, governance models, and human–AI collaboration. The findings underscore that businesses embracing this paradigm shift thoughtfully are well-positioned to unlock significant value from their data assets.
