Graph Neural Networks for Complex Enterprise Data Insights

Author: Pushkar Gupta

Journal: Global Business & Economics Journal (2025/07/05)

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24511935

Abstract

This article explores the application of Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) in analyzing complex and interconnected enterprise data. Unlike traditional machine learning methods that treat data points independently, GNNs leverage graph structures where entities are represented as nodes, and their relationships as edges, enabling more sophisticated modeling of dependencies. The paper discusses the theoretical foundations of GNNs and highlights their practical applications in diverse enterprise sectors such as fraud detection, recommendation systems, risk assessment, healthcare analytics, and AI-driven customer relationship management. Through real-world case studies, the article demonstrates how GNNs provide enhanced insights by capturing complex relationships within data, improving decision-making processes, and offering a unified approach to integrating heterogeneous data sources. Challenges related to computational complexity, data quality, and interpretability are also addressed, along with future directions for GNNs in enterprise AI applications. This work contributes to the growing field of GNN research and its transformative potential in enterprise data science.
